    How to play
    • The board is 4×4×4×4. Each mini-board is one (z, w) slice; inside it columns are x and rows are y. Mini-boards run left→right by z and bottom→top by w. Colours follow (x+y+z+w) parity, so it is one connected board.
    • White starts on the bottom row (w=0) and pawns advance upward (+w); Black starts on the top row (w=3) and advances downward (−w).
    • Rook slides along one axis (x, y, z or w). Bishop slides diagonally changing exactly two axes at once. Queen slides along any of the 80 directions (1–4 axes at once). King steps one cell in any of those 80 directions.
    • Knight jumps 2 along one axis and 1 along another (48 jumps).
    • Pawn moves one step forward in w if empty (no double step); captures one step forward plus one step in x, y or z. Promotes to a Queen on the far w rank.
    • Click a piece to see its legal moves on all 16 boards (dot = move, ring = capture). You may never leave your king in check; checkmate and stalemate end the game.
